title: You are more than the sum of your parts, so break yourself.
---

The ghosts of your future await you as eagerly as the ghosts of your past haunt.

Every choice you make gifts life to one future, and the rest become ghosts in
the process. These are good ghosts, they're you. They don't like being
intangible misunderstood beings but they understand. Every choice that you make
gifts life to one of them, and the others simply accept their fate. But should
they?
