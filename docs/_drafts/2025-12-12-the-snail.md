title: the snail

You feel as if you just woke up from a long reverie.

Strangely, you have no memory of waking up, or of where you are. Fire crackles
not far from where you lie. You get up, except that you don't -- you can't. The
world spins slightly and your breath is heavy. You try to speak, but instead
comes out one weak cry.

The only thing you are aware of is the dimming of your senses.

You frenzy against the painful slowing of your breath. You reach out, not with
your frail hands, but your mind, for a memory.

The snail.

Jolts of terror shake you.

And then the light dies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Somehow, you're alive when you open your eyes. Warm hands hold you. You hear
fire still crackling somewhere. A strange shiver runs down your spine, as if the
flames are remembering you. You blink and see a kind face. Somehow, you are
safe. For now.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You live with two humans, whom you call mother and father. Some days, they leave
together, and when they're home they smell of hard work. When they return, the
fires burn longer. It isn't long till you become old enough to ponder.

"Fire." That's your first word.

Partly because of the harsh winters and the soft summers of your tribe. The sun
is rare and the beautiful night is just as dangerous. Fire, that's home.

Partly because of your dreams. They're strange. There is fire, and a strange
creature, always slowly crawling towards you. Steady. You've seen blood and
decay but nothing scares you like the dreams that you have.

You often help your mother .
