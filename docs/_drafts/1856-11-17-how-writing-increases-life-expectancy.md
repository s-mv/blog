I have spent my life observing authors. They do not die easy.

The Ides of November had just passed. It was a blatantly humid day for the time
of the year, but I had a mission in mind. I lit a cigarette, and then threw it
to the road. Because I don't smoke. It just looks cool. My civic sense: down the
gutters.

But it didn't matter. I had tracked him down.

I walked towards the abandoned building.
