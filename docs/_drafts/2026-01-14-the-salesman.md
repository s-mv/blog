In the country of Lanthania lived a class of poor salesmen. Characterized by
balding heads and empty pot bellies, they were pitiable souls who lived on the
mere morsels that their deep coffers could hold. Their leader, The Salesman, a
saint loved and hated by many, had helped the nation many times in its need. At
no cost!

For the greater good of Lanthania the salesmen would endure their decrepit
poverty and try their best to uplift others instead, often selling goods for
cheap, and even giving away freebies.

But the day soon rose when the salesmen had to, with heavy hearts, get off their
seats. Hunger and scarcity had hit the nation, and the people of Lanthania had
grown so demanding that the salesmen had to sacrifice on their behalf. How
miserable it is to have nowhere to sit, but to offer your seat to someone else?
A tragedy. No, it was time for the poor salesmen to think for themselves.

News spread throughout the country that the salesmen were planning something
big, for the greater good of course. Crowds arrived from all over Lanthania to
hear what they had to say. And it wasn't much later that The Salesman walked out
to address the public:

"Today," he said, "my brothers, the salesmen, and I have come to the decision
that in order to run this country, we can only provide for a part of the
population."

Whispers rose from the crowds.

"We have decided to only sell our goods to the people from the west. It is
imperative for the success of our nation and its values."

The rest of the speech was truly glorious, but forgotten by most. As The
Salesman waved to get their attention, the whispers turned into words of
betrayal, and soon, fights broke out.

The Salesman smiled, handing out shovels, bats and even popcorn to his eastern
brothers.

"Works every time."
